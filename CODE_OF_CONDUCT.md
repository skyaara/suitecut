# Code of conduct

SuiteCut contributors must make the project safe to work on.

- Discuss the code and the observable behavior, not a person's ability or motives.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and accept corrections without hostility.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or publish private information about another
  contributor.
- Stop an interaction when someone asks you to stop.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or
permanently ban a participant whose behavior harms the project or its contributors.

Report conduct problems privately through the repository's
[security advisory form](https://github.com/skyaara/suitecut/security/advisories/new) and put
"Code of conduct" in the title. Include links or screenshots and say whether there is an immediate
safety concern. Maintainers will share the report only with people needed to review it.
